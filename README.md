# CJ-DARCL-ATTRITION-BU-FTL

Key takeaways:

1. Fleet Crew Attrition Analytics – Analysed 17,992 employee records to identify attrition trends, tenure patterns, and key workforce insights using Excel-based analytics.

2. Growing in South – Mapped 203 industrial plants across six southern states and developed a 100-point opportunity scoring framework incorporating capacity, freight potential, strategic fit, market opportunity, and route accessibility. Performed sensitivity analysis to validate the robustness of the prioritization model and identified the top industrial opportunities for business expansion.

3. FTL Market Deep Dive – Conducted an in-depth competitive benchmarking of India’s Full Truck Load (FTL) market by analysing competitor strategies, financials, service portfolios, and industrial focus to evaluate CJ Darcl’s competitive positioning
